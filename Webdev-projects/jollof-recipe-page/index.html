How to Make
Jollof Rice

A simple guide to making delicious, flavourful Nigerian jollof rice at home.

View the recipe

THE RECIPE

A taste of home

Nigerian jollof rice is a popular one-pot rice dish cooked in a rich tomato and pepper sauce. It is full of flavour and is perfect for family meals, celebrations, or a weekend treat.

Prep time20 minutes
Cook time40–50 minutes
Servings4–5 people
DifficultyEasy

WHAT YOU NEED

Ingredients

  • 3 cups of long-grain parboiled rice
  • 4 fresh tomatoes
  • 2 red bell peppers
  • 1–2 scotch bonnet peppers
  • 2 medium onions
  • ½ cup of vegetable oil
  • 2 tablespoons of tomato paste
  • 2–3 cups of chicken stock or water
  • 2 teaspoons of curry powder
  • 1 teaspoon of dried thyme
  • 2 seasoning cubes
  • Salt to taste
  • 1 bay leaf (optional)

LET'S GET COOKING

Instructions

  1. Prepare the pepper mix

    Blend the tomatoes, red bell peppers, scotch bonnet peppers, and one onion until smooth.

  2. Prepare the rice

    Wash the rice thoroughly and set it aside. This helps remove excess starch.

  3. Fry the tomato paste

    Heat the vegetable oil in a pot. Add the chopped onion and tomato paste, then fry for a few minutes.

  4. Cook the pepper sauce

    Pour in the blended pepper mix. Add curry, thyme, seasoning cubes, salt, and bay leaf. Cook until the sauce thickens and the oil begins to rise.

  5. Add the rice and stock

    Add the washed rice and enough chicken stock or water to cook it. Stir until everything is well combined.

  6. Allow it to cook

    Cover the pot and cook on low heat until the rice is tender and the liquid has been absorbed. Add a little water if necessary.

  7. Serve and enjoy

    Fluff the rice gently with a fork and serve hot with chicken, fried plantain, or your favourite side dish.

Chef's tip

Keep the heat low after adding the rice, and avoid adding too much water. This helps the rice cook evenly without becoming soggy.
